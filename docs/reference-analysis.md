# Reference analysis — One Love homepage

Internal build guidance. Not a proposal.

## The one useful thing from each reference

| Reference | Principle taken |
|---|---|
| **Caladan** | Inset, rounded full-bleed hero (16px frame) so the photo reads as a framed destination, not a banner. Hairline-separated editorial rows (title · copy · image) instead of icon cards. A two-tone heading (solid + muted) used for hierarchy. Quiet FAQ. |
| **Waveyu** | Approachable warmth: a friendly grotesk, sand-white backgrounds, rounded 20–28px image corners, small eyebrow labels, one confident accent colour on the CTA. Photography-led CTA band near the end. |
| **Yachting** | Navigation that disappears into the hero and returns as a solid bar on scroll-up. Generous vertical rhythm around large photos. Nothing on the hero except type, image and one action. |
| **Lifecycle** | Typography doing the layout work: large left-aligned headings, a ruled grid, a single strong paragraph set at display size. The bottom-right "secondary panel" on the hero (we use it for pricing). |
| **Avenora / Wayo** | Varied, asymmetric image grids for destination content; image cards with the title set over or under the photo in display type; tall + wide crops mixed in one grid. |
| **SaleUnion / Scalient** | Commercial clarity: one primary CTA colour site-wide, arrow-on-hover micro-interaction, price/figure typography that is large but calm, a compact proof element next to the CTA. |

## Principles actually used

1. **One palette, photo-led colour.** Sand `#F5EFE4` + paper white, ink navy `#0A1A3A` (derived from the logo's `#002060`), one accent: logo coral `#F04850` (darkened to `#D8333C` for white-on-coral AA). Logo yellow `#F8D858` only for rating stars and small highlights. The carts themselves supply the rainbow.
2. **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display, 600–800, tight tracking — youthful, with character at large sizes) + Instrument Sans (UI/body). Self-hosted WOFF2, latin subset, `font-display: swap`, display face preloaded.
3. **Rhythm, not repetition.** Sections alternate in density and tone: dramatic hero → typographic statement → product blocks → dark navy benefits → delivery list with photo → light three-step → editorial reviews → asymmetric Explore grid → quiet FAQ → full-bleed photo CTA. No two adjacent sections share a composition.
4. **Containers vary.** Full-bleed inset frames (hero, CTA), a 1320px editorial grid, and narrower measures for text. Not one 1200px box.
5. **Motion:** clip/translate reveals on headings and images (IntersectionObserver, transform/opacity only), slow image scale on hover, header hide/show on scroll, menu slide. All disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content is visible without JS.
6. **Mobile is recomposed:** hero type re-broken for 390px; cart blocks become a scroll-snap row with a peek; Explore grid becomes a horizontal rail; delivery rows compress to name + time; sticky bottom "Book now" bar on small screens.

## Deliberately not copied

- Caladan's in-hero booking form (the real form is WPForms on `/book-now/`; a fake one would mislead).
- Avenora's giant background word, Wayo's sun disc, Scalient's dark SaaS glow, SaleUnion's logo marquee and pastel blocks.
- Glass cards, gradient blobs, decorative grids, pill-badge clutter, giant stat numerals.
- An island-map concept. Delivery is a clear list; a proper illustrated map is a later asset.

## One Love asset limitations (these shape the design)

- **Logo** is a 300×110 PNG only. No vector, no light/reversed version (a reversed version was generated by recolouring the navy pixels — it needs to be replaced with a proper vector from the client).
- **Photography** is phone photography (max 1600×900, most 1280–1440px). It's good daylight colour with real carts in real San Pedro locations: Tropic Air terminal, the San Pedro park on Barrier Reef Drive, a guest convoy on the beachfront street, a colourful fleet line-up. It isn't sharp enough for 2× full-bleed retina heroes. Large crops are kept inset, overlaid and sized to the source.
- **No real destination imagery** (Secret Beach, the reef, the bridge, the north). The 2026 blog featured images look **AI-generated** and are used only as interim thumbnails on Explore cards that link to those guides. They are flagged for replacement.
- **No photos of staff, a handover or guests on arrival**. These are needed for trust and for the How-it-works section.
- Carts are mostly **lifted, multi-colour carts**, not the "white Club Car" the manual's alt-text examples describe. Alt text describes what is actually in each photo.

## Shot list that would materially improve the site

1. Portrait (4:5) and landscape (16:9, ≥2400px) hero: a branded One Love cart with guests on Barrier Reef Drive with the sea visible, golden hour.
2. A cart on the sand road at Secret Beach with turquoise water (≥2000px).
3. Handover moment at the SPR terminal or water-taxi dock: staff member, keys, guests with luggage.
4. Clean side-profile of a 4-seater and a 6-seater, same location/angle/lens, for an honest size comparison.
5. The Sir Barry Bowen Bridge with a cart crossing.
6. Team portrait (the family / Junior / Hani) outside the Barrier Reef Drive office.
7. Vector logo (SVG) in full-colour and reversed versions.
